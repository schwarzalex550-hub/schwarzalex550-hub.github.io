By using YouTube Uploader ("the app") you agree to this Privacy Policy. Please read it before signing in.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API) to upload videos and manage video details on YouTube channels that the user has authorized. By using the app you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy.

2. Information the app accesses

When an authorized company account signs in with Google and grants access, the app accesses only what is needed to upload and manage the company's own videos:

  • the ID, title and handle of the YouTube channel that is selected;
  • information about videos uploaded by the app (video ID, title, description, tags, category, language, thumbnail, privacy status, scheduled publishing time, upload and processing status);
  • the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google for that channel.

The app does not access Google account passwords, email contents, payment information, subscribers' personal data, or viewers' data.

3. How the information is used

The information is used only to upload the videos the company chooses, set their details as configured, and show the result of each upload. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics resale, or any other purpose.

4. How the information is stored

All information, including the OAuth tokens, is stored locally on the company's own computers. The app does not operate a server and does not upload data anywhere other than to YouTube itself, as part of the uploads requested.

5. Sharing

The information is not sold, rented or shared with any third party, internal or external, beyond the authorized company staff who operate the app. The only outside party that receives data from the app is YouTube/Google, as required to perform the requested uploads.

6. Advertising and cookies

The app does not show advertising and does not allow third parties to serve content or advertising. The app does not place, read or recognize cookies or similar technologies on users' devices or browsers. This website does not use cookies.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

Access to a Google account and YouTube channel can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

In addition to the app's normal procedure for deleting stored data, when access is revoked the stored tokens and all data obtained under that authorization are deleted from the company's computers as soon as possible and in any case within 7 days. The app periodically confirms that its authorizations are still valid and deletes data for any authorization that can no longer be refreshed within 30 days.

8. Security

Stored tokens are kept in files readable only by the operating-system user account that runs the app and are never published or transmitted to anyone other than Google.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app starts to access or use data in a way not described here, this policy will be updated and must be accepted again before the new use begins.
