YouTube Uploader

An internal tool our company uses to publish its own videos to its own YouTube channels.

What the app does

YouTube Uploader is a desktop application used internally by our company. It uploads videos that the company has produced to YouTube channels that the company owns or manages, and sets each video's title, description, tags, category, language, thumbnail, privacy status (public, unlisted or private) and scheduled publishing time.

The app uses the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services). Access to a channel is granted by an authorized company account through Google's standard sign-in and consent screen, and can be revoked at any time.

Who can use it

The app is not offered to the public. It is used only by authorized company staff for the company's own channels.
